# Product

FleetVellum: Financial intelligence for every fleet.

AI cost intelligence software that connects vehicle expenses, invoices and mileage records to help fleet managers understand operating costs.

- Founder and CEO: Kingsley Eze
- Co-founder and CTO: Temitope Ajayi
- Industry: Transportation, FinTech, Fleet Management
- Customers: Transportation companies, leasing businesses and logistics operators
- Core features: Expense extraction, cost classification, vehicle cost comparison, spending forecasts, discrepancy alerts
- MVP: Invoice uploads and vehicle cost dashboards
- Planned AWS: Textract, Bedrock, SageMaker AI, S3, DynamoDB
- Business model: Per-vehicle subscriptions
- Main action: Request a pilot
