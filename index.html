How a FleetVellum pilot runs

  1. Load the vehicle list and mileage

    We import your vehicle register and a mileage export from your trackers or logbooks.

  2. Upload a month of invoices

    Forward scans and PDFs; each invoice is read and matched to a vehicle within the hour.

  3. Review the cost dashboard

    Managers clear alerts and compare cost per kilometre across vehicles and depots.

Questions

What fleet teams ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

Which invoices can it read?

Scans, photos and PDFs of fuel, repair, tyre, toll and leasing invoices.

Does it pay or block invoices?

No. It flags costs that look wrong; your managers and finance team decide.

What does each vehicle cost?

A subscription per vehicle per month. We quote after the pilot.

Where does mileage come from?

Tracker exports, fuel card data or driver logbooks, as CSV or Excel.

Can leasing businesses use it?

Yes. Lessors can compare costs across the vehicles they lease out.

How long until the first dashboard?

About a week after your vehicle register and first month of invoices arrive.
